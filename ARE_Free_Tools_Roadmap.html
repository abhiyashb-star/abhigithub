Step 3
🎵 Free Music + Real Alpha-Wave Layer
🧠 Real Neuroscience Technique

Left earphone 200 Hz + Right earphone 208 Hz = brain perceives 8 Hz alpha wave (difference tone). Actual meditation apps use this. Tumhari BGM literally audience ke brain waves affect karegi.

Step 1 — Generate Base Music

Suno AI — Free Tier (50 songs/day)

Prompt: "cinematic ambient, dark orchestral, no vocals, 8-bit cosmic texture, 140 BPM, suitable for sci-fi documentary"
Generate 3–4 versions, pick the best. Download as MP3.

Step 2 — Add Binaural Layer (Audacity)

Free: Audacity + Generate Tones

1. Open MP3 in Audacity
2. Add new stereo track → Generate → Tone
3. Left channel: 200 Hz, Sine wave, amplitude 0.08
4. Right channel: 208 Hz (for 8 Hz alpha), amplitude 0.08
5. Mix down → Export as WAV/MP3

Step 5
💡 Prompt Engineering — 8K Feel on Free Tools

Universal prefix

Har prompt ke shuru mein add karo:
cinematic 8K, IMAX quality, film grain, anamorphic lens, shallow depth of field,
Ye consistently better results deta hai free models pe bhi.

Scene 1 (Spark Fall)

Single glowing ember falling slowly through infinite darkness, golden light trail, cinematic macro, extreme slow motion, black background, no camera movement

Scene 4 (Point Cloud)

3D point cloud materializing from darkness, teal and cyan particles, depth map visualization, slow rotation, LIDAR-style scan, sci-fi documentary aesthetic

Scene 6–7 (Act II)

Digital glitch art, corrupted data stream, cold blue desaturated palette, cyberpunk dystopian, flickering neon, 2001 Space Odyssey aesthetic, no warmth

Scene 9 (Galaxy Zoom)

Hyperspace jump, stars rushing past, galaxy center zoom, Kubrick inspired, no music cues, pure visual spectacle, warm amber star trails, infinite depth

Negative prompts (always add)

Add to every prompt:
no watermark, no text, no logo, no blur, no artifacts, no stock footage look, no generic corporate aesthetic

Regeneration strategy

Generate 3 versions of each scene. Pick the best one. If none work, change ONE word in the prompt — usually "cinematic" → "documentary" or "macro" → "wide angle".

Hailuo credit trick

Generate 3-second clips (uses fewer credits). Then use CapCut's AI slow-motion to stretch to 6 seconds. Output looks smoother AND uses half the credits.
